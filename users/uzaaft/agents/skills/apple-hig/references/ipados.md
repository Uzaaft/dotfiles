# Designing for iPadOS

Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/designing-for-ipados

## Platform characteristics

1. Display: large, high-resolution screen suited for richer layouts.
2. Ergonomics: hand-held, desk, and stand usage with varying viewing distance.
3. Inputs: Multi-Touch, keyboard, trackpad or mouse, Apple Pencil, and voice, often combined.
4. App sessions: quick interactions and long productivity or creative sessions.

## iPadOS system features to account for

1. Multitasking
2. Widgets
3. Drag and drop

## Best-practice checklist

1. Use large-screen real estate to emphasize content and reduce full-screen modal disruptions.
2. Place controls within easy reach while protecting content visibility.
3. Tune content density based on viewing distance and current input mode.
4. Support combined input workflows, including keyboard plus pointer and Apple Pencil.
5. Adapt to orientation changes, multitasking modes, Dark Mode, and Dynamic Type.
6. Ensure the design can transition naturally to macOS contexts when applicable.

## Implementation notes

1. Prefer layouts that scale to split-view and stage-like multitasking contexts.
2. Support drag-and-drop affordances in content and file-management areas.
3. Use input-agnostic interactions so touch, pointer, and keyboard paths remain consistent.
